This dataset shows protected wreck sites, vessel wrecks and seabed archaeological features in UK territorial waters. 

Use it to check whether a site falls within a protected wreck area. These areas are designated heritage assets and have the same level of protection as scheduled monuments and highly graded listed buildings.
